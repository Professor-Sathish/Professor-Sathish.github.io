About

I'm Prof. Sathish Ramanujam

R. Sathish, M.E., (Ph.D.) - Head - ADS (Artificial Intelligence and Data Science) at the School of Innovation, KGiSL Institute of Technology, Coimbatore, and Head of the School of Programming Excellence.

I work in computer vision, deep learning and multimedia forensics, and have submitted my Ph.D. thesis in Machine Learning to Anna University. I build practical AI systems in Python, from face detection and deepfake video analysis to real-time pose estimation, and bring that hands-on work into teaching, student mentoring and technical events. Before teaching, I worked as a Malware Analyst at Dell.

Research

Doctoral research - Anna University

Adaptive Hybrid Deep Learning Framework for Enhanced Deepfake Video Detection via Feature Integration

A three-part pipeline: an Enhanced Viola-Jones face detector, a hybrid spatio-temporal network (AHMcRAN) that learns manipulation patterns, and an improved training optimizer (ICLO). It outperformed CNN and ResNet baselines by 6.1% and 11.4% on Celeb-DF.
